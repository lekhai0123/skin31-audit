Graphical abstract: Label errors and source-modality confounding in a composite skin disease benchmark.

(a) The audit identified 158 reference-diagnosis contradictions among 1,511 ISIC-derived images and detected train-test lesion overlap for 105 of 305 ISIC-derived test images (34%). The clinical images illustrate atlas-derived classes using openly licensed ISIC clinical close-ups; they are not images from the benchmark atlas. (b) Clinical and dermoscopic views of one lesion illustrate the paired evaluation design. The illustrated pair is histopathology-confirmed basal cell carcinoma (ISIC lesion IL_7279125; images ISIC_6874409 and ISIC_4385084). The distributions are schematic; +68 percentage points is the registered seven-output P1 estimate for the primary fusion-distilled B0 model on Derm7pt. (c) Bars show overall P1 on Derm7pt for B0 with training optimisation, averaged over three seeds: corrected benchmark A, 0.55; adding MILK10k clinical images B, 0.61; adding dermoscopic images of the same lesions C, 0.35. Nevus dermoscopic recall decreased from 0.92 in B to 0.76 in C.
